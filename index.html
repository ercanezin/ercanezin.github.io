Welcome

Dr. Ercan Ezin is a tenured Lecturer in Computer Engineering at Harran University, Türkiye, currently working as an AI Expert at the Council of Higher Education (Yükseköğretim Kurulu – YÖK) in Ankara. He is a researcher at the ANLAM-NET Lab (Advanced Natural Language, Multimedia and Network Laboratory) at Ankara University.

His current research interests span natural language processing, large language models, multimodal AI, recommendation systems, and explainable/reliable AI for real-world applications. His work emphasises rigorous evaluation, practical deployment considerations, and the development of data-driven systems that support human decisions in areas such as marketing, tourism, education, and software-intensive domains.

Before joining academia full-time, Dr. Ezin worked across industry roles including Java/software engineering, data science, full-stack development, and IT management. He holds an MSc in Advanced Computer Science from the University of Essex, UK, and dual BSc degrees, in Software Engineering and in Computer Education and Instructional Technologies, from Bahçeşehir University, İstanbul.

Research Focus

  • NLP & Large Language Models

    Sentiment analysis for low-resource languages, LLM-assisted annotation, and multi-agent LLM reasoning.

  • Multimodal AI

    Controlled evaluation of text and image models and the score discrepancies between modalities.

  • Recommender Systems

    Context-aware and group recommendation for tourism, and algorithmic personalisation in marketing.

  • Reliable & Explainable AI

    Reliability, verifiability, and leakage-aware validation of AI systems used for decision support.
